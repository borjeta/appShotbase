Shotbase support

This page is the official support for the Shotbase app, published by Borja Alventosa Julià. Last update: 18 November 2026. This is an English translation of the Spanish original.

What Shotbase is

Shotbase is an iPhone app that turns your screenshots into a searchable database: it reads the text of each screenshot, classifies it (recipe, event, place, Wi-Fi, coupon or code, conversation, product, note...), extracts its data and lets you find it with natural language. From each screenshot you can add an event to your calendar, create a reminder, open an address in Maps, join a Wi-Fi network or copy a piece of data. Everything is processed on your iPhone; it has no accounts and no ads. It is free up to 50 processed screenshots and offers an optional subscription, Shotbase Pro (monthly or annual), to process them all. It requires an iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Frequently asked questions

Shotbase does not process my screenshots. What should I check?

  1. Photos permission. In Settings › Permissions › Photos access it should say "Full" or "Limited". If it says "Denied", tap "Open iPhone Settings" and allow access to Photos. With limited access, Shotbase only sees the screenshots you have selected: use "Choose more screenshots" to extend the selection.
  2. Free plan limit. The free plan processes up to 50 screenshots. If you have reached it, the app tells you; with Shotbase Pro all of them are processed. The ones already processed remain available.
  3. Time. Shotbase processes pending screenshots while the app is open and, when iOS allows it, in the background with the iPhone plugged in. In Settings › Library you can see how many are processed and pending.
  4. Screenshots with errors. If Library shows "Failed", tap "Retry failed ones".

My screenshots show as "Only in iCloud"

If you use "Optimize Storage" in Photos, some screenshots may be only in iCloud and Shotbase cannot read them on your iPhone. Turn on Settings › Privacy & data › Download from iCloud if needed and tap "Retry failed ones" in Library. The download is done by Photos (the system), not by Shotbase, and may use data and time. It is off by default.

My iPhone does not have Apple Intelligence, or it is turned off

Shotbase still works: without Apple Intelligence it uses its own rules to classify and extract data. It still finds your screenshots by their text, but it understands some screenshots less well and cannot answer searches with a sentence. In Settings › Intelligence you can see Apple Intelligence's status and turn its use on or off. If you turn it on later, tap "Re-analyze all" to repeat the analysis. The model runs on your iPhone: nothing leaves the device.

Shotbase got a piece of data wrong (date, code, password...)

Text recognition and data extraction can fail, especially with blurry, heavily cropped or text-dense screenshots. Open the screenshot and check the data in "Recognized text" and in the full-size image before relying on it. Shotbase can make mistakes: always verify what matters. You can let us know at borjawebspubli@gmail.com (without sending us sensitive data).

How do the actions (calendar, reminder, Maps, Wi-Fi) work?

  • Add to calendar: appears if Shotbase detects a date. The system event editor opens, pre-filled; you decide whether to save it. No Calendar permission is needed, and Shotbase does not read your calendar.
  • Create reminder: iOS asks you for Reminders permission the first time. If you denied it, turn it on in iPhone Settings › Shotbase. Shotbase only creates the reminder; it does not read the ones you already have.
  • Open in Maps: opens the Maps app with the address or place. Shotbase does not make that lookup; Maps does.
  • Join this Wi-Fi: appears on screenshots with a Wi-Fi network. iOS asks for confirmation before connecting and the network is saved on your iPhone; you can forget it in Settings › Wi-Fi.
  • Copy: copies the data (password, code, address, text...) to the clipboard.

What is Spotlight and how do I turn it off?

By default, Shotbase adds your screenshots to your iPhone's search index (Spotlight), so you can find them from iOS search. A title, the type, the date and, depending on the type, extracted data and the first 500 characters of the recognized text are indexed; for Wi-Fi screenshots the extracted password is not added. Full details in the privacy policy, section 5.1. If you prefer them not to appear, turn off Settings › Privacy & data › Show in Spotlight: Shotbase removes the entries it had added. We recommend turning it off if you take screenshots with sensitive information.

Can I search from Shortcuts or Siri?

Yes: the app includes the "Search my screenshots" action (Shortcuts and Siri). It opens Shotbase with the search already done, which is processed on your iPhone.

How many screenshots can I process for free?

Up to 50 processed screenshots. With Shotbase Pro there is no such limit.

What does Shotbase Pro include? Is it a subscription?

Yes, it is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or annual, that lets you process all your screenshots. Prices in Spain: €2.99 per month or €19.99 per year (the equivalent in local currency elsewhere); there is no free trial. You see the final price on the purchase screen, as shown by Apple, before confirming. It is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Full terms in the Legal notice (Spanish), section 5. Terms of use: Apple's standard license agreement (EULA).

How do I cancel the subscription?

From iPhone Settings › your name (Apple ID) › Subscriptions › Shotbase. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period so it does not renew. Cancellation is handled by Apple, not by the developer. You keep Shotbase Pro until the end of the period already paid, and your already-processed screenshots remain available; deleting or uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Settings › Restore purchases in the app, or the "Restore" button on the Shotbase Pro screen. Sign in, if asked, with the same Apple ID you subscribed with, and you need an internet connection. If it does not appear, check that you are using the right Apple ID and that you are online, and write to us with the Apple receipt (the "Apple Receipt" email).

I changed iPhone or reinstalled the app: do I lose Pro?

No. The subscription belongs to your Apple ID, not to the iPhone. On the new iPhone, install the app, sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID and tap "Restore purchases". What does not carry over is the app's data (recognized text, extracted data, settings): Shotbase will process your screenshots again. On iPhone, if you restore the device from an iCloud or computer backup, the app's data may come back.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer, and the developer cannot issue them directly. Request it at https://reportaproblem.apple.com (sign in with your Apple ID, choose the option to request a refund, the reason and the purchase). Apple decides. If you are a consumer in the EU, your statutory rights (such as the right of withdrawal, with the exceptions provided by law) are not limited: see the Legal notice (Spanish), section 5.

What data does the subscription use?

Apple processes the payment (the app does not see your payment details). RevenueCat, a third-party service acting as the developer's processor, checks and restores the subscription with an anonymous customer identifier, your purchase history and subscription status, the IP and technical data of your device and the app. There is no account and no tracking. Details in the privacy policy, section 6.

What permissions does the app ask for and why?

  • Photos (required): reading your screenshots so you can search them. It only queries screenshots, not the rest of your photos, and never deletes or modifies photos.
  • Reminders (optional): only when you tap "Create reminder".
  • Calendar: no permission; the system event editor lets you save the event.
  • Wi-Fi (optional): only when you tap "Join this Wi-Fi"; iOS asks for confirmation.

You can withdraw any permission in the iPhone settings. Without it, only the features that need it stop working. The app does not use location, camera, microphone or contacts.

How do I delete my data?

  • Uninstalling the app removes Shotbase's local data (recognized text, extracted data and settings). To be sure that no Spotlight entry remains, turn off Show in Spotlight first.
  • Turning off Spotlight (Settings › Privacy & data) removes the entries Shotbase added to the system index.
  • Deleting a screenshot from Photos also removes its record and its Spotlight entry in Shotbase when syncing.
  • Your original screenshots remain in Photos: Shotbase does not delete or modify them.
  • Subscription data at RevenueCat: write to us at borjawebspubli@gmail.com and we will handle it with RevenueCat.

Keep in mind that iCloud or computer backups may contain the app's data; review them in your device settings. More detail in the privacy policy, sections 3 and 9.

How do I report a problem with the app?

Write to borjawebspubli@gmail.com stating: iPhone model, iOS version, app version, what you were doing and what you expected to happen. Do not include sensitive data in the message (passwords, codes or the content of your screenshots). The app does not automatically send error reports to the controller, so we will not know about the problem unless you tell us.
