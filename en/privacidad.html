1. Who is the controller

The controller responsible for this policy is Borja Alventosa Julià, an individual ("the controller"), who publishes the iPhone app Shotbase ("the app"). The controller's identification details are in the Legal notice (Spanish).

2. Summary

  • The app has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics of its own, shows no ads and does not track you: it uses no advertising networks and does not ask for the iOS "tracking" permission.
  • All the work on your screenshots happens on your iPhone. Shotbase reads your screenshots from the Photos app (with your permission), recognizes their text (Apple's OCR), classifies them and extracts their data (with the Apple Intelligence model that runs on the device itself, if your iPhone has it, or with the app's own rules) and builds an index so you can search them. None of this is sent to the controller or to any server, and the images of your screenshots are not copied out of Photos.
  • There is an optional subscription, "Shotbase Pro" (monthly or annual), which removes the free plan's screenshot limit. Apple processes the payment (the app never sees your payment details) and RevenueCat, a third party acting as the controller's processor, manages the subscription: it processes an anonymous customer ID generated by RevenueCat, your purchase history and subscription status, your IP address and technical device and app data. It is not used for tracking or advertising. Details in section 6.
  • RevenueCat is the only network connection the app itself makes. Shotbase makes no other internet connection by itself. Some system features that you turn on or tap (opening an address in Maps, downloading from iCloud a screenshot that is only stored there) are performed by iOS and are explained in section 5.
  • The recognized text of your screenshots and the extracted data may include anything that appears in them (conversations, addresses, passwords, codes, personal data). They are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local database (section 3).
  • Spotlight is on by default: Shotbase adds a title, the type and part of the text of your screenshots to your iPhone's search index. You can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & data › Show in Spotlight (section 5.1).
  • You can delete your data by uninstalling the app, turning Spotlight off or deleting the screenshot from Photos (section 3). Shotbase never deletes or modifies your photos.

How to delete this data

  • Delete the screenshot from Photos (or remove it from the selection, if you use limited access): when syncing with your library (when you open the app and when iOS notifies it), Shotbase deletes that screenshot's record and its Spotlight entry.
  • Settings › Privacy & data › Show in Spotlight: when you turn it off, Shotbase removes from the Spotlight index the entries it had added.
  • Uninstall the app: iOS removes the app's local data (the database and settings). To be sure that no Spotlight entry remains, turn off "Show in Spotlight" first. Also check your backups (section 9).
  • Revoke the Photos permission (iPhone Settings › Shotbase › Photos): Shotbase stops reading your screenshots, but the data already stored stays in the app until you uninstall it.
  • Your original screenshots are not touched: they remain in Photos. Shotbase does not delete or modify them.
  • Subscription data already held by Apple and RevenueCat is not on your device: see section 6.

5. System features involved

These features are performed by iOS, not by Shotbase. Shotbase makes no network request by itself except the subscription one (section 6).

5.1. Spotlight (on by default)

  • What it is: the system's local search index (Core Spotlight). iOS stores it on your iPhone; Shotbase does not send it to any server. It is on by default and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & data › Show in Spotlight; when you do, Shotbase removes the entries it had added.
  • What Shotbase adds: the screenshot's title, its type, its date, keywords (the type, "Shotbase" and "captura") and a description that depends on the type:
    • for Wi-Fi screenshots, only the title (normally the network name): Shotbase does not add the extracted password;
    • for coupon or code screenshots, only the store and the discount (Shotbase does not add the code);
    • for event or ticket screenshots, the title, the venue and the address (no booking reference and no recognized text);
    • for all other types (recipe, place, conversation, product, note or other), the extracted data (for example titles or addresses) and the first 500 characters of the recognized text.
  • Keep in mind: whatever appears in a screenshot may end up in Spotlight (for example, a password or a code written in a conversation or a note), and anyone with your unlocked iPhone could see it when searching. If you take screenshots with sensitive information, turn Spotlight off.
  • When you tap a Spotlight result, the screenshot opens in Shotbase.

5.2. iCloud download (off by default)

If you have "Optimize Storage" turned on in Photos, some screenshots may be only in iCloud. By default, Shotbase does not read them and marks them "Only in iCloud". If you turn on Settings › Privacy & data › Download from iCloud if needed, Shotbase asks Photos to download them; the download is done by the system (the Photos app), not by Shotbase, which does not connect to iCloud or send anything. The app does not sync its own data with iCloud.

5.3. "Open in Maps"

If you tap "Open in Maps" on a screenshot with an address, a place or a mention, Shotbase asks the system to open an Apple Maps link (maps.apple.com) with that text, and the Maps app handles it. Shotbase does not make that network request; subsequent processing is done by Apple under its policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/.

5.4. Copy and Shortcuts

  • Copy: the "Copy" actions (password, code, address, text...) put that text on the system clipboard only when you tap them. Other apps may read the clipboard under iOS rules.
  • Shortcuts and Siri: the app offers "Search my screenshots". Shotbase only receives the search text the system gives it and processes it on your iPhone.

5.5. Background processing

When iOS decides to, it can run the processing of pending screenshots while the app is closed. The app requests this only for when the iPhone is plugged in to power and without requiring an internet connection: this work sends nothing to the network (unless you turned on iCloud download, section 5.2, which is done by the system).

5.6. Links and this website

"Privacy policy" and "Terms of use" in the app open your device's browser; the app sends no data about you when opening them. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical connection data of visitors (for example, the IP address) under its own policy.

6. Shotbase Pro subscription (Apple and RevenueCat)

6.1. What is offered

Shotbase is free up to 50 processed screenshots. Shotbase Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or annual, that lets you process all your screenshots without that limit. Prices in Spain: €2.99 per month or €19.99 per year (the equivalent in local currency elsewhere); there is no free trial. The final price is shown on Apple's purchase screen, in your currency, before you confirm. You do not need to subscribe to use the screenshots already processed. Full terms in the Legal notice (Spanish), section 5.

6.2. Who does what

  • Apple charges and manages the payment, your Apple ID, cancellations and refunds. The app does not see or store your payment details (card, payment method, billing name). Apple processes that data under its own policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (a third-party service, with servers in the US) acts as the controller's processor: it checks your subscription status with Apple and restores it when you ask. According to its policy, RevenueCat is not the controller of this end-user data and acts as a processor. Its privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Its data processing agreement (DPA): https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa/.
  • The controller (section 1) decides to use RevenueCat for this feature. It does not receive your payment details nor can it tell who you are from the anonymous identifier.

6.4. Retention

The controller does not store this data on any server of its own. RevenueCat retains it under its policy and its DPA: its policy states that, for end-user data, it processes it only as long as necessary to comply with its legal obligations; under the DPA, it keeps it until its agreement with the controller ends, and destroys or returns the data if the controller requests it (with exceptions for its backups). Apple retains your purchase data under its policy. The subscription itself belongs to your Apple ID, not to the app.

6.5. How to restore the subscription

  • Restore purchases: if you already subscribed (for example, after reinstalling the app or changing iPhone), open Settings › Restore purchases in the app, or the "Restore" button on the Shotbase Pro screen, and sign in, if asked, with the same Apple ID you subscribed with. There is no app account: the subscription is tied to your Apple ID. An internet connection is required.
  • Uninstalling and reinstalling: the same: restore the subscription with the same Apple ID. You do not need to pay again.

6.6. International transfers

According to its privacy policy, RevenueCat stores data on Amazon Web Services servers in the US. For transfers of personal data from the European Economic Area, its DPA relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with a UK addendum and Swiss-specific terms) and lists its sub-processors, located in the US (including AWS). Apple processes data under its policy. The controller does not transfer data by itself.

6.7. Your rights over subscription data

Since there are no accounts, the controller cannot link the anonymous identifier to you on its own. If you want to access this data or request its deletion, write to borjawebspubli@gmail.com; to locate your record at RevenueCat, the anonymous identifier of your installation or details of your purchase (for example, the Apple receipt) may be needed. The controller will handle the request with RevenueCat, which under its DPA assists the controller with these requests and usually refers anyone who writes to it directly back to the controller. If your RevenueCat record is deleted, the subscription will still exist on your Apple ID. You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD) (section 10).

6.8. Cancellation and refunds

These are handled by Apple, not by the controller or the app. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, in iPhone Settings › your name (Apple ID) › Subscriptions. Cancelling does not delete the data on your iPhone. Refunds are requested at https://reportaproblem.apple.com (see Support and the Legal notice in Spanish).

7. What does NOT happen

  • There are no accounts, registration or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, advertising networks or tracking across apps or websites.
  • There is no analytics of its own, nor error reports sent to a server of the controller.
  • Your screenshots, their recognized text, the extracted data and the search index are not sent to any server or external AI: classification and extraction are done by Apple's model running on the iPhone itself or by the app's rules.
  • Shotbase does not delete or modify your photos.
  • The app does not see or store your payment details: Apple processes them.

8. General legal basis, minors and automated decisions

8.1. Legal basis (for guidance)

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and applicable Spanish law only regulate the processing of personal data. For guidance:

  • Data stored only on your device: the controller neither receives nor accesses it, so it does not process it as a controller. You control that data and can delete it whenever you want (section 3).
  • System features (Spotlight, iCloud download, Maps): they happen because you turn them on or request them; they are performed by iOS or Apple under their own policy.
  • Subscription (Apple and RevenueCat): see section 6.3. Processing by RevenueCat is done on behalf of the controller, as processor.
  • International transfers: the controller does not itself transfer personal data to third countries, because it does not receive it. RevenueCat and Apple may have servers outside the European Economic Area; see section 6.6.
  • Automated decisions and profiling: the controller does not carry them out. Classification of your screenshots by type happens on your iPhone, only serves to organize them and has no legal effects on you.

8.2. Minors

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not offered as a children's app. Anyone under 14 (the minimum age to consent to data processing in Spain) should not use it without the authorization of their parents or guardians. The app does not ask for age or personal data, has no accounts and shows no ads. Purchases are handled by Apple with the controls configured on the Apple ID and the device (for example, "Ask to Buy" in Family Sharing). If you believe a minor has given consent without authorization, write to borjawebspubli@gmail.com.

9. System backups

Depending on your configuration, iCloud backups or backups made on a computer may include the app's local data, including the database with the recognized text of your screenshots. That is managed by Apple and you, not by the controller; Shotbase does not exclude its database from those backups. If you do not want it included, review your device's backup settings. The subscription does not depend on these backups: it is recovered with "Restore purchases" (section 6.5).

10. Your rights and supervisory authority

Since the controller does not receive your data, the most direct way to exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability is to use the app and the system themselves (everything is on your device and you can view it, and delete it as described in section 3). For subscription data processed by RevenueCat as the controller's processor, write to the controller (section 6.7); payment and Apple ID data are handled by Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/). If you have questions or want to exercise any right against the controller, write to borjawebspubli@gmail.com.

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD): https://www.aepd.es, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.
